Angry Birds is one of the most successful and recognisable mobile games ever created. Developed by Rovio Entertainment and first released in 2009, the game quickly became a worldwide phenomenon. Its simple but addictive gameplay, colourful characters, and humorous style attracted millions of players across the globe. The game revolves around a group of birds who are angry because a gang of green pigs has stolen their eggs. The player's objective is to help the birds defeat the pigs and recover their stolen eggs. The gameplay of Angry Birds is easy to understand but can be challenging to master. Players use a slingshot to launch birds at structures built by the pigs. These structures are made from different materials such as wood, stone, and glass. Each bird has unique abilities that can be used strategically. For example, the Blue Bird can split into three smaller birds, while the Black Bird explodes on impact. Players must carefully aim their shots and make use of each bird's special abilities to destroy the pigs and complete levels. One of the reasons for Angry Birds' success was its accessibility. The game could be played by people of all ages and required no previous gaming experience. Its levels started off simple but gradually increased in difficulty, encouraging players to improve their skills. The game's physics-based mechanics made every shot feel satisfying, especially when players managed to bring down an entire structure with a single bird. The characters in Angry Birds became extremely popular. Red, the main bird, is known for being determined and brave. Other birds, such as Chuck, Bomb, and the Blues, each have distinct personalities and abilities. The green pigs also became iconic villains due to their comical appearance and mischievous behaviour. These memorable characters helped Angry Birds become more than just a game. As the popularity of Angry Birds grew, Rovio expanded the franchise into many forms of media. Numerous sequels and spin-off games were released, including Angry Birds Space, Angry Birds Star Wars, and Angry Birds 2. The franchise also inspired toys, clothing, books, and animated television series. In 2016, The Angry Birds Movie was released, introducing the characters to an even wider audience. A sequel followed in 2019. The impact of Angry Birds on the gaming industry was significant. It demonstrated the potential of mobile gaming at a time when smartphones were becoming increasingly common. The game's enormous success inspired many developers to create similar mobile games and helped establish mobile gaming as a major part of the entertainment industry. Today, Angry Birds remains one of the most famous game franchises in the world. Although many new mobile games have appeared since its release, Angry Birds is still remembered for its innovative gameplay, memorable characters, and influence on gaming culture. Its combination of fun, strategy, and humour has ensured its place as a true classic in the history of video games.
